# Fit Minds

## Overview

Fit Minds is a health and fitness planning application designed to help college students and adults manage their wellness while balancing busy schedules. The app focuses on three important areas: fitness, sleep, and reliable health information.

The idea behind Fit Minds came from user interviews that revealed how difficult it can be to maintain healthy habits while managing classes, work, commuting, and everyday responsibilities.

Rather than providing a one-size-fits-all fitness plan, Fit Minds aims to make health planning more flexible, understandable, and personalized.

## The Three Deep User Needs

Fit Minds was designed around three core user needs identified through research and interviews:

1. **Flexible Fitness Planning:** Users need fitness routines that adapt to changing class, work, and personal schedules because rigid workout plans are difficult to maintain when daily commitments change.

2. **Understanding Sleep Quality:** Users need to understand their sleep quality beyond simply knowing how many hours they slept because sleep duration alone does not always reflect how well they rested.

3. **Reliable Health Information:** Users need trustworthy health and fitness information because inaccurate or poorly supported information makes it difficult to make informed decisions about their health and track meaningful progress.

## Key Features

- **Personalized Fitness Routines:** Generate weekly workout schedules based on user availability, preferred workout frequency, and selected workout types, including cardio, strength, and bodybuilding.
- **Sleep Tracking:** View and log sleep information to better understand sleep duration and quality.
- **Health Information & Sources:** Explore fitness and cardiovascular health topics, supporting references, and information about the reliability and limitations of health research.
- **User Profile:** View personal information and schedule details used to personalize the experience.

## Project Goal

The goal of Fit Minds is to make healthy habits easier to maintain by helping users build realistic fitness routines, understand their sleep, and access reliable health information—all in one application.

Demo login: **taylor / fitminds123**. "Reset demo" on Home restores the sample data.

## Testing on a real phone
1. Phone and PC must be on the same Wi-Fi.
2. Run `npm run dev` and open the **Network** URL it prints (like http://192.168.x.x:5173) in Safari/Chrome on the phone.
3. Tap User ID or Password: the phone's own keyboard opens. (Chrome DevTools "device mode" and Figma prototypes do NOT show a real keyboard.)
